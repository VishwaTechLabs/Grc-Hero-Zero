### 🛠️ Practical execution workflow

```text
1️⃣ DEFINE SCOPE
      ↓
2️⃣ UNDERSTAND BUSINESS PROCESS
      ↓
3️⃣ IDENTIFY ASSETS / DATA / DEPENDENCIES
      ↓
4️⃣ IDENTIFY RISK
      ↓
5️⃣ DEFINE CONTROL OBJECTIVE
      ↓
6️⃣ WRITE TESTABLE CONTROL
      ↓
7️⃣ COLLECT SYNTHETIC EVIDENCE
      ↓
8️⃣ TEST DESIGN + OPERATION
      ↓
9️⃣ RECORD FINDING / RESULT
      ↓
🔟 REMEDIATE OR ACCEPT WITH AUTHORITY
      ↓
1️⃣1️⃣ CALCULATE / EXPLAIN RESIDUAL RISK
      ↓
1️⃣2️⃣ REPORT TO MANAGEMENT
```

### 📌 How to know you achieved the objective

You are **Done** only when you can answer all seven:

- [ ] What business objective are you protecting?
- [ ] What risk are you addressing?
- [ ] Which control addresses the risk?
- [ ] Who owns the risk and control?
- [ ] What evidence proves operation?
- [ ] How would an auditor test it?
- [ ] What decision is required if the control fails?

### 🧪 Practical output standard

Your final artifact should contain:

```text
INPUT
 ├── Business context
 ├── Scope
 ├── Assets / data
 └── Requirements

PROCESS
 ├── Risk analysis
 ├── Control design
 ├── Evidence collection
 └── Control testing

OUTPUT
 ├── Result
 ├── Finding / gap
 ├── Remediation
 ├── Residual risk
 └── Executive summary
```

### 🔍 Quality gate

| Gate | PASS condition |
|---|---|
| Scope | Clear and bounded |
| Risk | Written as cause → event → impact |
| Control | Specific and testable |
| Evidence | Identifies what/when/who/result |
| Test | Repeatable procedure |
| Finding | Condition + criteria + cause + effect |
| Remediation | Owner + action + due date |
| Residual risk | Reassessed after treatment |
| Communication | Understandable by management |

### 🎓 Instructor review

Ask the student:

> **“Show me the evidence.”**

Then ask:

> **“Which risk does this evidence prove you are controlling?”**

Then:

> **“What would happen to the business if this control failed?”**

This prevents framework memorization and builds real GRC reasoning.
