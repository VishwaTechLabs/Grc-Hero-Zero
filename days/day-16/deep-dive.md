# 📘 Day 16 Deep Dive — Third-Party Risk

## Executive explanation
Third-party risk is risk introduced through suppliers, SaaS, cloud providers, contractors and strategic partners. Teach tiering, inherent vendor risk, due diligence, questionnaires, contracts, security/privacy clauses, evidence, continuous monitoring and exit planning.

## 🧠 Mental model

```text
BUSINESS OBJECTIVE
      ↓
CONTEXT / ASSETS
      ↓
RISK SCENARIO
      ↓
CONTROL OBJECTIVE
      ↓
CONTROL ACTIVITY
      ↓
EVIDENCE
      ↓
TEST
      ↓
FINDING / EFFECTIVE
      ↓
REMEDIATION
      ↓
RESIDUAL RISK
      ↓
EXECUTIVE DECISION
```

## Key vocabulary
- **vendor tiering** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.
- **due diligence** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.
- **contract controls** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.
- **continuous monitoring** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.
- **exit plan** — define it in your own words and give one enterprise example.

## What students must be able to do
1. Explain the concept to a non-technical executive.
2. Write a risk statement.
3. Design a control.
4. Identify reliable evidence.
5. Test the control.
6. Write a finding if the control fails.
7. Recommend treatment.
8. Explain residual risk.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a framework as a checklist.
- Writing controls that cannot be tested.
- Confusing evidence with a screenshot.
- Treating an issue as a risk.
- Assigning a control owner without accountability.
- Closing findings without remediation evidence.

### 📌 How to know you achieved the objective

You are **Done** only when you can answer all seven:

- [ ] What business objective are you protecting?
- [ ] What risk are you addressing?
- [ ] Which control addresses the risk?
- [ ] Who owns the risk and control?
- [ ] What evidence proves operation?
- [ ] How would an auditor test it?
- [ ] What decision is required if the control fails?

### 🔍 Quality gate

| Gate | PASS condition |
|---|---|
| Scope | Clear and bounded |
| Risk | Written as cause → event → impact |
| Control | Specific and testable |
| Evidence | Identifies what/when/who/result |
| Test | Repeatable procedure |
| Finding | Condition + criteria + cause + effect |
| Remediation | Owner + action + due date |
| Residual risk | Reassessed after treatment |
| Communication | Understandable by management |

### 🎓 Instructor review

Ask the student:

> **“Show me the evidence.”**

Then ask:

> **“Which risk does this evidence prove you are controlling?”**

Then:

> **“What would happen to the business if this control failed?”**

This prevents framework memorization and builds real GRC reasoning.
